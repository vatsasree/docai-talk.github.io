The problemWhy grounding
The status quo — and where it cracks

Today, grounding = the LM writes geometry as text

Fine for a rectangle. Now do the same surgery on a curved river:

Too many tokens

a faithful outline costs hundreds of coordinates — per span

Nothing ties them to the page

coordinates are just more next-token predictions — they can be hallucinated

Evidence is plural

real answers cite several places at once; one geometry string per answer doesn’t scale

so — divide and conquer

Let the language model do what it is good at — decide what the evidence is — and let a segmentation model do what it is good at: draw it. One network, trained end-to-end. Not a pipeline.

What we must handle — and what we’d like

Evidence has any shape

curved rivers, pie wedges, skewed scans — a rectangle cannot trace them

→ ground with pixel masks

a mask takes exactly the shape the evidence has

Evidence is plural

one answer often cites several places — a logo and three table rows

→ one mask per cited span

as many [GROUND]s as the answer needs — zero is fine too

Scope depends on the use

cite a number · redact a row · chunk a section — three different “wheres”

→ three granularities, nested

phrase ⊂ line ⊂ block, from the same token

No model — and no dataset — did all three. That is the gap M3Grounder fills.

Paper 1 · Training

Two document priors the losses must know

Don’t spill off the text

a bleed-suppression loss penalizes every bit of mask that lands off the text

The three masks must nest

an enclosure loss makes phrase ⊂ line ⊂ block — the coarser mask is the finer one’s ground truth

Both are trained jointly with the usual text + segmentation losses — together they are worth +5 F1 on grounding.

Paper 2 · Training stage 2 · why

SFT imitates one chain. The model can write many.

Sample the SFT model twice on the same question — both answers are “160 ≠ 150”:

chain A — right, and shows it

S₁ the Net Sales column [GND] → the column ✓
S₂ values 20 · 20 · 20 · 50 · 30 · 20 [GND] → six cells ✓
S₃ they sum to 160 — printed total 150 [GND] → the total ✓

chain B — right answer, broken trail

S₁ the Gross Sales column [GND] → wrong column ✗
S₂ the values total 160 [GND] → mask on the logo ✗
S₃ …so the answer is no — right, for the wrong reasons

SFT treats both the same — it never compares them. Preference optimization is exactly that comparison.

Paper 2 · Training stage 2 · how

DPO reads those two chains as text

𝓛DPO = −log σ( β [
log πθ(chain A | I, q) πref(chain A | I, q)
the better chain — pushed up
−
log πθ(chain B | I, q) πref(chain B | I, q)
the worse chain — pushed down
] )

π is the LM head’s probability

of writing that chain, token by token — the steps, the words, the [GND]s. θ = the model being tuned · ref = its frozen SFT copy.

So only the VLM moves

every term is text likelihood → gradients reach only the 🔥 VLM. GIM and the segmenter appear nowhere → ❄ frozen.

Why no mask term?

a preference needs a likelihood; the decoder emits one mask, not a distribution over masks — so mask preferences are ill-posed.

The catch — πref leashes the text. Nothing leashes the hidden [GND] states that the frozen mask pipeline reads. Next slide.

Paper 3SAM, in one slide
Paper 3 · The question

So — SAM for documents?

Run the same recipe on pages instead of photos. Each ingredient has a document-shaped answer:

TASK

Hierarchical, open-set, promptable layout segmentation — a page + a prompt in; a layout tree, a pixel mask per node, and (on request) an explanation out.

MODEL

DocLayout-VL — a VLM that writes the tree, a segmenter that draws the masks, and a hierarchy-aware module between them.

DATA

HOPLay-Docs-80M — 2.5M pages · 80M masks · 400K open-set labels · 40 languages, engine-generated like SAM’s.

The rest of this section walks those three — and why documents need one more idea SAM never did: hierarchy.

Layout analysis is stuck at closed-set boxes

Why should attention follow the tree?

A node’s meaning lives in its neighbourhood: a headline belongs to its article, a caption to its figure. The footer ad has nothing to say about either.

HGC

Structure becomes locality. The mask is rebuilt from the tree the model itself just generated — its own structure prediction constrains its grounding.
