What comes from LISA, and what is new

LISA · Lai et al., CVPR 2024

Reasoning segmentation on natural images.

One token → one mask, trained end to end with text + mask losses. Followed by LISA++, GLaMM, PixelLM — all on natural scenes.

“SAM’s prompt interface can be driven not by human-provided points or boxes but by learned prompt embeddings derived from the VLM’s hidden states.”

What documents forced us to add

Transfer fails. These models “do not transfer well to documents, where interpretation depends on text layout, reading order, and the semantic structure of graphical elements.” So: a document data engine, 2M grounded QA, no hand-drawn masks. (LISA++ and GLaMM, even fine-tuned on our data, reach 25.4 / 35.2 F1 on GDQA-Bench — ours 79.0; supplement.)
Evidence is plural. One answer, K pointers — K chosen by the model.
Evidence has scope. Three nested masks per pointer: phrase ⊂ line ⊂ block.
Text has priors. Don’t bleed off the text; finer masks nest in coarser ones — as losses.
…and the next two papers add pointers that agree with each other across the steps of a chain, pointers that survive preference training, and one pointer per node of a tree.

Two document-specific losses

Don’t spill off the text

a bleed-suppression loss penalizes every bit of mask that lands off the text

The three masks must nest

an enclosure loss makes phrase ⊂ line ⊂ block — the coarser mask is the finer one’s ground truth

Both are trained jointly with the usual text + segmentation losses — together they are worth +5 F1 on grounding.

Why preference training?

Sample the SFT model twice on the same question — both answers are “160 ≠ 150”:

chain A — right, and shows it

S₁ the Net Sales column [GND] → the column ✓
S₂ values 20 · 20 · 20 · 50 · 30 · 20 [GND] → six cells ✓
S₃ they sum to 160 — printed total 150 [GND] → the total ✓

chain B — right answer, broken trail · hands up if you would trust it

S₁ the Gross Sales column [GND] → wrong column ✗
S₂ the values total 160 [GND] → mask on the logo ✗
S₃ …so the answer is no — right, for the wrong reasons

SFT never shows the model two of its own chains and says which is better. Preference training is exactly that comparison.

AQ — the answer matches · CQ — the chain’s steps match the reference (F1) · GQ — masks right: IoU ≥ 0.5, right element type, right step

Paper 2 · Training stage 2 · background

From preferences to DPO

a preference

p(yw ≻ yl | x) = σ( r(x, yw) − r(x, yl) )

Bradley–Terry: a hidden reward r explains which of two outputs is preferred

RLHF would fit a reward model and run RL against it, leashed to the SFT model by a KL term. But that problem has a closed-form optimum, π* ∝ πref · er/β — so the reward can be read off the policy itself. Substitute it back and the constant cancels:

DPO

𝓛DPO = −𝔼 log σ( β log πθ(yw)/πref(yw) − β log πθ(yl)/πref(yl) )

a classification loss on two chains’ log-probabilities — the reward model you would have trained is already inside the policy. No reward model, no RL.

For a token sequence, log π is a sum of next-token log-probabilities — the loss touches exactly what the language-model head writes.

Preference training, on the text

𝓛DPO = −log σ( β [
log πθ(chain A | I, q) πref(chain A | I, q)
the better chain — pushed up
−
log πθ(chain B | I, q) πref(chain B | I, q)
the worse chain — pushed down
] )

π = probability of writing that chain

token by token — steps, words, and the [GND] tokens. DPO shapes where the model grounds, though it never sees a mask.

So only the VLM moves

every term is text → the mask pipeline appears nowhere → ❄ frozen. Unfreezing it would give it no gradient; the only lever is the states that feed it.

Why no mask term?

a preference needs a likelihood — the decoder emits one mask, not a distribution.

The catch — this leashes the text. Nothing leashes the hidden states the frozen mask pipeline reads.

Why should attention follow the tree?

A node’s meaning lives in its neighbourhood: a headline belongs to its article, a caption to its figure.

HGC · Hierarchical Grounding Contextualizer

Structure becomes locality. After the tree is written, a small transformer refines only the [LAY] states, with attention shaped like the tree the model just wrote; the refined states prompt the segmenter.

Containment
